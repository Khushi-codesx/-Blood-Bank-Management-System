Frequently Asked Questions

Who can donate blood? ▼
Healthy individuals aged 18-65 years, weighing at least 50 kg, and with normal hemoglobin levels can donate blood.
How often can I donate blood? ▼
You can donate whole blood every 3 months (12 weeks). Platelet donation can be done more frequently.
Is blood donation safe? ▼
Yes, it is completely safe. Sterile and disposable equipment is used for each donor, so there is no risk of infection.
How long does the donation process take? ▼
The entire process takes about 30–45 minutes, while the actual blood donation takes only 8–10 minutes.
Does donating blood hurt? ▼
You may feel a small pinch when the needle is inserted, but the process is generally painless.
What should I do before donating blood? ▼
Eat a healthy meal, drink plenty of water, and avoid alcohol or smoking before donation.
What should I do after donating blood? ▼
Rest for a few minutes, drink fluids, avoid heavy exercise for 24 hours, and keep the bandage on for a few hours.
Can women donate blood? ▼
Yes, women can donate blood if they meet the eligibility criteria and are not pregnant or anemic.
Can I donate blood if I have a medical condition? ▼
It depends on the condition. People with certain illnesses or infections may not be eligible. Always consult the medical staff.
How much blood is taken during donation? ▼
Around 350-450 ml of blood is collected, which your body replaces within a few days.
Can I donate blood on an empty stomach? ▼
No, it is recommended to eat before donating to avoid dizziness or weakness.
Why is blood donation important? ▼
Blood donation helps save lives during emergencies, surgeries, and for patients with serious illnesses like anemia or cancer.
Will I feel weak after donating blood? ▼
Most people feel fine, but some may feel slightly tired. Drinking fluids and resting helps recovery.
Can I donate blood if I have a tattoo? ▼
Yes, but usually only after 6–12 months of getting the tattoo, depending on guidelines.
Is there any benefit of donating blood? ▼
Yes, it helps maintain iron levels, improves blood flow, and gives a sense of helping others.
